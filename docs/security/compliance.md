# Compliance Notes

## Principles

* Respect website Terms of Service and `robots.txt`.
* Comply with applicable data-protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) as the project scales.
* Maintain auditability and user control.

## Data Protection

* Personal data collected only with user consent.
* PII encrypted at rest and in transit.
* Users can export and delete their data (Phase 13).
* Data retention limits defined per data type.

## Automation Ethics

* No CAPTCHA, MFA, or anti-bot circumvention.
* No automatic submission where not permitted.
* No scraping of private contact information.
* No bulk unsolicited messages.

## Website Terms Compliance

* Each `JobSource` records whether automation is allowed.
* Discovery respects crawl rates and `robots.txt`.
* Browser automation stops when prohibited behavior is detected.

## Record Keeping

* Audit log captures:
  * Profile changes.
  * Approvals and rejections.
  * Application and outreach actions.
  * Source policy checks.
  * Security events.

## Future Compliance Work

* Privacy policy and terms of use before public registration.
* Cookie consent if analytics cookies are used.
* Legal review of Level 3 automation and recruiter outreach.
